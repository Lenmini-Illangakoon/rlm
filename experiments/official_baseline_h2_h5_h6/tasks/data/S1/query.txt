Reconcile the records in the sections labelled === Orders ===,
=== Shipments ===, and === Refunds ===. Section end markers delimit their scope.
Orders contains exactly 100 unique orders. Each record has a unique Record ID
and an explicit Order ID; quantities are positive integer unit counts.

Match records only on exact Order ID, never customer name, record position, or
a nearby or similar-looking identifier. Ignore shipment/refund records whose
Order ID is absent from Orders. Count each distinct Record ID once; repeated
Order IDs in Shipments or Refunds represent separate records, not duplicate
orders. All matching records count subject to the following status rules.

Order Status is open or cancelled. Shipment Status is posted or void: sum only
posted Shipped Quantity and exclude void shipments. Refund Status is approved
or pending: sum only approved Refunded Quantity and exclude pending refunds.
Refunded Quantity explicitly counts units, not money. Missing matching
shipments or refunds contribute zero to their respective totals.

For each open order, outstanding units = Ordered Quantity minus the sum of
posted Shipped Quantity minus the sum of approved Refunded Quantity. Select
all open orders whose outstanding units are strictly positive. Exclude every
cancelled order even if its balance is positive, and exclude fully reconciled
orders with zero outstanding units. Valid posted shipments plus approved
refunds never exceed Ordered Quantity.

Return the complete selected Order-ID set in ascending order and the total
outstanding units across that set. Both exact membership and the total are
required; harmless formatting differences do not matter.
